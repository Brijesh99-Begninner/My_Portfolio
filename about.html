BIOGRAPHY

Who Is Brijesh?

I am a goal-oriented Front-end Developer with a passion for web design and building modern, creative user interfaces. I graduated in 2024 with a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) degree from Rashtrasanth Tukdoji Maharaj Nagpur University.

My professional focus lies in building clean, responsive, and visual web interfaces. I enjoy transforming complex design mockups into semantic, lightweight HTML and CSS layouts. I believe that front-end development is the perfect intersection of logic, usability, and design.

Currently, I work as a Front-end Developer at Kartexin Technologies Private Limited. Before this, I supported international booking platforms with WNS Pune, during which I managed operational customer queries and large-scale data workflows under tight SLAs.
